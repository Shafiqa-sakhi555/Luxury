# Returns Policy

**Source:** `src/lib/infoPages.ts` (returns) | **Verified:** partial — `placeholder: true` until client legal review

## Return eligibility

### Standard products

Ready-made rugs, cushions, décor and stocked furniture **may** be returned within **7 days** of delivery if:

- Unused
- In original packaging

Contact your branch or email **orders@jalalsgroup.com** to initiate a return.

### Non-returnable products

- Custom-sized or made-to-measure items (unless manufacturing defect)
- Items cut, sized or personalised to customer specifications
- Used or damaged by customer after delivery

Customer must acknowledge non-returnability before confirming custom orders.

## Return period

**7 days** from delivery (standard products only).

## Required evidence

For damaged items: photos within **48 hours** of delivery.

## Return process

1. Contact branch or orders@jalalsgroup.com
2. Provide order number and reason
3. Await approval and return instructions
4. Item inspected on receipt
5. Refund or replacement per refund policy

## Approval process

Handled by branch or orders team — not automated via chatbot. Assistant can explain policy but cannot approve returns.
