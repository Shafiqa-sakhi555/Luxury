## Who is Jalal?

Jalal's Home Solution is a premium home furnishings and surfaces retailer serving homeowners across Gilgit-Baltistan and Pakistan. The business operates showrooms under Jalal's Home Solution, Jalal Carpets, and Pak Turk Carpets brands.
